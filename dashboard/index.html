        Simulated sends the I3 frame to a badge-sim over local TCP — no radio.
        Real LoRa transmits it over the mesh: Dell nugget → battery relay → HP
        junction + badge. Switching re-provisions the badge on the new link (~15 s for the
        mesh). The dashboard holds no key and no payment power either way.

        The dashboard holds no key and no payment power.
